## Design Specificity Verdict
Colours are on-brand, but the structure is a generic admin template. The nav is not grouped by the product's two pillars and the shell shows no gap signal.

Detector: 9 CLI findings. Real: #d97f37 at side-bar.component.scss:77, and the 9px label at side-bar.component.scss:40. False positives: broken-image (Angular [src] binding) and 4 inherited rgb(0,0,0) text hits.
Browser overlay: 10.5px company subtitle, 9px "MAIN" label, flat type hierarchy (1.20:1 steps), transition:all on layout. Skeleton marquee flag is a false positive.

## Priority Issues
- [P0] Users and Reports unreachable by keyboard; collapsed rail has no accessible names (menu-item.component.html:2-13). Fix: parent items as buttons with aria-expanded, aria-current on links, sr-only labels plus tooltips when collapsed, nav landmark. /impeccable harden
- [P1] Submenus empty when the page loads collapsed (side-bar.component.ts:77-105); label-based role filter diverges from routes. Fix: menu as computed() over roles and a fixed tree; visibility from route data.roles. /impeccable harden
- [P1] Topbar controls lie or no-op: quarter select is a TODO (header.component.ts:59-62), subtitle range stale, controls shown on Settings, no Refresh feedback, unlabeled picker. Fix: computed subtitle, per-route control scope, Refresh busy state plus "Updated" time, picker ariaLabel. /impeccable clarify
- [P1] Phone shell broken: clipped title, 3-line subtitle, squeezed picker, hidden sidebar still in tab order, no focus trap or Escape. Fix: inert closed sidebar, focus trap, Escape to close, toggle label and aria-expanded, second row for controls on phones, one-month picker. /impeccable adapt
- [P2] Contrast and token drift: "MAIN" label ~2.3:1, cool-grey subtitle ~3.5:1, avatar ~3.0:1, nav text 50% vs 70% spec, sidebar 256 vs 200px, dead PanelMenu preset. /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags
- Alex (power user): no shortcuts, range presets or "last refreshed" time; Users and Reports always take two clicks.
- Sam (keyboard and screen reader): can't reach Users or Reports; collapsed rail announces unnamed links; no nav landmark or aria-current; unlabeled picker; no h1; hidden sidebar links tabbable on phones; overlay has no focus trap or Escape.
- Hana (HR lead): Users submenu empty below 1024px; subtitle shows the old range; Refresh never confirms; "Unexpected Error." x3 with blank cards.

## Minor Observations
- Initials take the first two letters of the name; email breaks mid-domain.
- transition:all animates width and margin; animate transform only.
- marginTop 64 duplicates --topbar-height.
- Logo alt text sits inside an aria-hidden span; toggle image alt duplicates the button label.
- .btn-ghost:focus should be :focus-visible.
- Titles come from a URL if-chain instead of route data.
- Shell components missing OnPush.
- "/" redirects Business and ProjectManager users to /dashboard, which their roles can't access.
- Error toasts cover the topbar controls.
